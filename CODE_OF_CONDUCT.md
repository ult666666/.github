# Code of conduct

These projects follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the work. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and sharing others' private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, reviews or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior through [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). For a private conversation with the maintainer, open a minimal issue asking for a private contact route. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may block contributors who repeatedly or severely violate it, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
